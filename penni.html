Privacy Policy for Penni

Last updated: July 29, 2026

Welcome to Penni ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring your personal financial information remains confidential and secure. This Privacy Policy outlines how your data is collected, used, and safeguarded when you use the Penni mobile application.

1. Information Collection and Storage

Personal Financial Data: Penni is designed with a privacy-first approach. All financial data you enter—including income, expenses, custom categories, monthly budgets, and notes—is stored locally on your device's internal storage. We do not transmit, upload, or store your personal financial data on external cloud servers.

2. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

Penni uses Google AdMob to display advertisements within the application. AdMob may collect and use pseudonymous device identifiers and other usage data to serve personalized or non-personalized advertisements.

Information collected by Google AdMob may include:

  • IP address
  • Device identifiers (e.g., Advertising ID)
  • Location data (coarse/country level)
  • App performance and crash logs

For more details on how Google processes your information, please review the Google Privacy & Terms and Google AdMob Policies.

3. Data Usage

Your local financial data is used exclusively within the app to render budget progress bars, analytical charts, weekly spending habits, and currency configurations. None of your ledger or budgeting information is accessible to third parties or advertising partners.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Since all financial data is saved locally on your device, you maintain full control over your data. You can delete your financial records at any time within the application settings or by clearing the app data/uninstalling Penni from your mobile device.

5. Children's Privacy

Penni does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate steps.

6. Security

We value your trust in using Penni. While no method of electronic storage is 100% secure, we rely on standard Android application security frameworks and local sandboxing to protect your device data.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app updates or regulatory changes. You are advised to review this page occasionally for any changes. Changes are effective immediately upon being posted on this page.
