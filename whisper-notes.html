Privacy Policy

App Name: Whisper Notes

Developer: Suresh Dahal

1. Overview

Your privacy matters. Whisper Notes is built as an offline-first, privacy-focused note-taking app. We do not collect, sell, or share your personal data.

2. Data We Collect

a) Notes and Content

  • All notes are stored locally on your device.
  • We do not read, analyze, or upload your notes without your permission.

b) Google Account (Optional)

  • You may optionally sign in using Google.
  • Google Sign-In is used only to identify the user for backup purposes.
  • We do not access your email content, contacts, or other Google services.

c) Google Drive Backup (Optional)

  • If enabled, your notes are backed up to your own Google Drive.
  • Backup frequency is controlled by you (manual, daily, weekly, or disabled).
  • Backup data is never stored on the developer’s servers.

3. Data Storage and Security

  • Notes remain on your device unless backup is enabled.
  • App lock (PIN or biometric) helps protect local access.
  • Whisper Notes does not operate backend servers.

4. Data Sharing

We do not:

  • Sell user data
  • Share data with third parties
  • Use advertising SDKs
  • Use analytics or tracking tools

5. Permissions Used

  • Storage access – for exporting or restoring notes
  • Biometric / lock access – to secure the app
  • Internet access – only for Google Sign-In and Drive backup

6. Children’s Privacy

Whisper Notes does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided personal data, please contact us.

7. User Control

  • Use the app without logging in
  • Enable or disable backup at any time
  • Export or delete your notes whenever you choose

This Privacy Policy may be updated in the future. Any changes will be posted on this page.
