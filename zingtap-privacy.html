Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-23

ZingTap ("we", "our", or "us") provides the ZingTap mobile application. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service.

1. Information Collection and Use

ZingTap is designed as a digital business card sharing tool. We prioritize your privacy by keeping your data local whenever possible.

  • Profile Information: You may provide your name, phone number, email, and company details to create your digital card. This information is stored locally on your device.
  • Scanned Contacts: Information you receive from other users via NFC or QR code is stored in a local database on your device (History). We do not upload this data to external servers.

2. Third-Party Services

Our app uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you:

  • Google Play Services: Required for app functionality and distribution.
  • Google AdMob: We use AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect data such as your Device ID and location to serve personalized ads. You can manage ad preferences in your Google Account settings.

3. Data Storage

All contact data you create or scan is stored within the app's internal database on your smartphone. If you uninstall the app, this local data will be deleted unless you have backed it up using system-wide Android backups.

4. Permissions

To provide its core features, ZingTap may request the following permissions:

  • NFC: Required to share and receive digital cards via tap.
  • Contacts (Optional): If you choose to "Save to Phone," the app will request permission to write to your device's contact list.
  • Internet: Required to load advertisements and fetch app updates.

5. Security

The security of your data is important to us, but remember that no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure. We use standard Android security practices to protect your information on your device.

6. Children's Privacy

Our Service does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
